Delete your QR Artistic account

You can permanently delete your account directly in QR Artistic: Account → Sign out → Delete account permanently.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the app, email KThinking.app@gmail.com with the subject: “QR Artistic – Account deletion request”.

Please send the request from, or include, the email address linked to your Google or Apple account so we can verify ownership.

What is deleted

We delete your account, generated QR images, generation and scan history, coin balance, and reward history.

Data retained

Content reports and limited pseudonymous anti-fraud records may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. We will explain any applicable retention period when processing your request.
